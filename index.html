Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

Can we use just one part of the service?

Yes. Tell us which task or area you need help with. We can discuss a defined piece of work or an ongoing management arrangement, subject to availability and an agreed scope.

What types of organisation can use your services?

Supported-accommodation providers, organisations setting up a service and property owners working with providers can contact us. Tell us about your requirements so we can confirm whether we are the right fit.

Can we discuss volunteer recruitment or coordination?

Yes—tell us what you are planning. We will confirm whether this can be included in the service and agree responsibilities for recruitment, suitability checks, safeguarding, supervision and coordination before any work begins.

Do you carry out statutory safety inspections?

We coordinate checks and assessments through appropriately qualified professionals. A management agreement does not automatically transfer a landlord’s or provider’s legal responsibilities; these must be understood and clearly allocated.

How much does the service cost?

Fees depend on the tasks, number of properties and level of ongoing coordination required. We discuss your needs and provide a written scope and quote before work starts.
